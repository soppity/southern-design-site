# Keyword research from OpenSEO, 4 November 2026

Source: OpenSEO project "Southern Design" (US market, Google). Monthly US search volume, keyword difficulty (KD, 0 to 100) and cost per click.
About 370 credits used. This research replaced the earlier judgement-based choice of target terms.

## What southerndesign.com ranks for today

19 keywords in total, all of them the company name or unrelated names ("southern made", "southern disposal mena ar").
No product, service, manufacturer or problem search brings it any traffic.

## Who wins today

- Pump type searches: manufacturers' and distributors' explainer and product pages (March Pump, PSG Dover, Carotek's vertical turbine page at 6). An explainer plus the brands supplied is the format that ranks.
- Industrial pump repair: regional repair shops with a city in the title (Houston, San Diego, Michigan). Needs the shop details Southern Design has not yet supplied.
- Pump cavitation: CSI (a distributor) ranks first. Distributors can win problem searches.
- Carotek ranks for about 760 non-brand keywords, mostly through one page per manufacturer, one page per product series or model (Wilden, Viking H32, Flexaseal cartridge seals), brand repair pages ("grundfos pump repair") and buying guides ("seal pots", "fix leaking pump seals").
