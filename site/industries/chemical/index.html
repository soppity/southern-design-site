Pumps, seals and valves for chemical plants

What the duty demands

Chemical service is defined by what must not escape. Liquids are corrosive, hazardous or both, so containment and material compatibility come before everything else. The common failures are dry-run mag-drive pumps, elastomers attacked by the liquid, and seals working in conditions they were not chosen for.

What we supply

  • Sealless pumps: Finish Thompson ETFE-lined and Richter PFA-lined mag-drive pumps
  • Liquiflo gear pumps for metering and transfer
  • Diaphragm pumps from Graco and Nomad
  • Mechanical seals and seal support for sealed pumps
  • Richter PFA-lined control, relief and sampling valves
  • Power monitors for dry-run protection

Problems we are called about

  • Why mag-drive pumps fail: dry running, decoupling and solids
  • Why does my mechanical seal keep failing?
  • Water hammer and pulsation in pump systems: causes and fixes
