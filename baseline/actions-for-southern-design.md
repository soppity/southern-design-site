| Thomasnet, Manta, Yellow Pages, Yahoo Local | 304 Kenova Rd, Smackover | Thomasnet shows the zip as 72762 |

El Dorado looks like the current address and Smackover the old one, but nothing found gives a move date. **Southern Design needs to confirm which is right.**

Maryland has a smaller version of the same problem:

- The website and the Google listing say 8229 Cloverleaf Drive, Suite 430, Millersville.
- Yelp shows 403 Headquarters Dr, Millersville.
- Pentair's Myers distributor listing for Maryland shows the Smackover address and the phone number 410-987-7867.

Once the addresses are confirmed:

1. Correct the Arkansas Google business listing, add opening hours, and choose categories that say what the business is (pump supplier, industrial equipment supplier).
2. Ask a handful of Arkansas customers for a Google review. The Maryland listing has three; Arkansas has none.
3. Ask EBARA, Liquiflo, Richter, MTH and Pentair to update the address and replace mysoutherndesign.com with southerndesign.com.
4. Claim and correct the Thomasnet, Manta, Yelp and Yellow Pages entries.

- Where pump repairs are carried out for each territory, what the shop can handle (lifting capacity, balancing, test stand) and typical turnaround. The three state repair pages have a marked gap waiting for this.
