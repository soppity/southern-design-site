Ending unpredicted machine failures

A product supplier and a reliability partner in one company.

What we do

Southern Design supplies pumps, mechanical seals, valves and the accessories that keep them running, and provides the repair, field service and reliability engineering that find out why equipment fails. For over 20 years our people have provided sensible solutions to the industrial community.

Our philosophy

Southern Design is dedicated to being the premier industrial product provider. Our formula for success is providing superior products and services with superior people. Our commitment to solving the most difficult problems is what sets us apart.

Where we work

From a corporate office in El Dorado, Arkansas and an East Coast office in Millersville, Maryland, we serve Arkansas, Maryland, DC, Delaware, Virginia and the Carolinas.
