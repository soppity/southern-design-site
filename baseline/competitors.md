# Who ranks instead of Southern Design, and why

Checked on 4 October 2026. This looks at the pages that hold the top Google positions for the 19 local and service searches in `queries.csv`, and at what those pages have that Southern Design's current site does not. The ranking pages are listed in `2026-10-04/ranking-pages.csv`.

## What this is and is not

It is a page-by-page comparison built from Google results, the competitors' own pages and Google's business listings. It is not a full keyword or backlink report: how many searches each competitor ranks for in total, how many sites link to them and how much traffic they get can only be measured with a paid tool such as Ahrefs or Semrush.

## Why they rank and Southern Design does not

1. **They have a page that matches the search.** Each one has a page whose title, heading and address say the state and the service. Southern Design's current home page is titled "HOME | SD&M Main", has no main heading, about 150 to 180 words, and never uses the words "pump repair" or "distributor". Arkansas and Maryland each appear once, in the address.
2. **Length is not the reason.** Tencarva's Arkansas page is under 300 words and still ranks first for four of the six Arkansas searches. What it has is the state in the title and address, three real branches, and a large site behind it.
3. **Size.** Tencarva lists 35 locations in 15 states and 22 repair shops. Cummins-Wagner covers nine regions through its own branches and subsidiaries. Each branch page and each state adds to the whole site's standing. Southern Design is smaller and will not out-size them.
4. **Other sites list them.** Several top results are not the competitors' own sites but pages about them: manufacturers' "find a distributor" pages (Cat Pumps and ARO list Tencarva, AESSEAL lists Advanced Fluid Technologies) and directories (Thomasnet, GlobalSpec, Yelp, MapQuest). Those listings rank in their own right and also link back to the distributor.
5. **A page per manufacturer.** Carotek, Cummins-Wagner and Geiger each have a page for every line they carry, and those pages rank for "[brand] distributor [state]" searches.
6. **Google business listings.** Southern Design's Maryland listing is in good order (Millersville address, opening hours, three reviews). Its Arkansas listing shows 304 Kenova Rd, Smackover, with no hours and no reviews, while the website gives 2710 North West Avenue, El Dorado. It did not come up in a Google places search for industrial pump repair in El Dorado; Tencarva, Pumps & Power and Bayou Pumps did.

For the "how do I fix this" searches (cavitation, seal failure, reliability programmes) the top places go to trade bodies and publishers (pumps.org, Pumps & Systems, Reliabilityweb), manufacturers (John Crane, Wilo, March Pump) and a few distributors with articles (Carotek, CSI, Hayes Pump). Distributors can rank there, but the competition is national.

## What the new site already answers

- A page per state, per service and per manufacturer (32), each with the state in the title: points 1 and 5.
- Eight problem guides: the informational searches.

## What it still lacks, and who has to supply it

| Gap | Why it matters | Needs |
|---|---|---|
| The Arkansas Google listing address does not match the website | Mixed addresses weaken local results, and the listing is how map results are won | Southern Design to confirm the right address and fix whichever is wrong |
| Reviews and hours on the Arkansas listing | Empty listings rank below complete ones | Southern Design |
| Listings on manufacturers' distributor pages | They rank themselves and link back; Southern Design already appears on Ebara's | Southern Design to ask each manufacturer |
| Thomasnet and GlobalSpec profiles | Both appear in the top three for Arkansas and Maryland supplier searches | Southern Design to claim or create them |
| Repair pages for a named place ("Pump repair in Arkansas", "Pump repair in Maryland") | The service page covers every territory at once; competitors win with one page per state | Real detail from Southern Design: where repairs are done, what the shop can handle, turnaround |
| Facility photos, shop equipment, named people | The Baltimore repair page that ranks first is built from exactly this | Southern Design |

## Realistic expectation

The new site should be able to compete for "[manufacturer] distributor [state]" searches, for local searches once the Google listings are fixed, and for the problem guides over time. Outranking Tencarva and Cummins-Wagner for the broad state searches ("pump distributor Arkansas") is harder because of their size, and is not something the site alone will decide.
