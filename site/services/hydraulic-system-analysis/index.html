Hydraulic system analysis and pump system design

Computer hydraulic simulation of your pumping and piping system, used to design it properly, select the right equipment, and find out why an existing system is not performing.

What it delivers

  • Proper system design
  • Equipment selection matched to the real duty
  • Reliability optimization: pumps running where they are meant to run
  • Troubleshooting of pumping and piping systems

Problems it settles

  • A pump that runs off its curve and nobody is sure whether to trim, slow or replace it.
  • Cavitation where the suction-side fix is not obvious.
  • Water hammer on long lines or with several pumps and valves.
  • A new line or an expansion where the pump has not been chosen yet.

What we need from you

  • Piping layout: lengths, sizes, fittings and elevations
  • Liquid properties and temperatures
  • The flows and pressures the process needs
  • Pump curves and current operating readings for an existing system

Where we provide it

Across Southern Design's territories, from offices in El Dorado, Arkansas and Millersville, Maryland.

Frequently asked questions

Is simulation worth it for one pump?

If that pump is a repeat failure or the fix involves buying equipment, yes. It costs less than buying the wrong fix.

Can you model an existing system?

Yes. Troubleshooting existing pumping and piping systems is one of the main uses.
