Should you repair or replace an industrial pump?

Short answer

Repair when the pump suits its duty, parts are available and the failure has a cause you can fix. Replace when the pump is wrong for the job, when the repair cost approaches the price of a new unit, or when waiting for parts will keep you down longer than a replacement would. The pump's repair history decides it more than the single failure in front of you.

Five questions that decide it

QuestionPoints to repairPoints to replace
Is it the right pump for the duty?It runs near its best efficiency point and the materials suit the liquidIt runs off its curve or the materials are being attacked
What does its history say?Long runs between failuresA bad actor with several failures in two to three years
What will the repair cost?Well under half the price of a new pumpApproaching or above half. Price both
How long are parts?On the shelf or days awayLong lead time or obsolete
Can the repair include an upgrade?Yes: seal, bearing protection, baseplate, materialsNo upgrade would change the outcome

Start with the duty, not the damage

A pump that is wrong for its service will fail again however well it is rebuilt. Check where it runs on its curve and whether the wetted materials suit the liquid before deciding anything.

Look at the history

One failure after years of service is a repair. The fifth failure in 18 months is a pattern, and the money spent so far belongs in the comparison. If you do not have the history to hand, that is the first thing to fix; see how to calculate MTBF and find bad actors.

Cost and lead time

A common rule of thumb is to price a replacement once a repair passes about half the cost of a new pump. Add the cost of downtime while parts arrive. For common ANSI process pumps, Hisco supplies complete pumps and parts to fit Goulds, Flowserve and Summit models, which often shortens the wait.

Upgrade while it is apart

  • Change a component seal for a cartridge seal.
  • Replace lip seals with bearing isolators.
  • Move to a corrosion-resistant baseplate if the old one has gone.
  • Correct the impeller diameter if the pump has been running off its curve.

What a good repair includes

A written inspection with measurements and photographs, the cause of failure, fits and clearances restored, the rotor balanced, and a record that goes into the pump's history. Southern Design's pump repair is done this way.

Frequently asked questions

Is it worth repairing a pump that keeps failing?

Only if the repair also removes the cause. Otherwise you are paying for the same failure again.

Can a pump be upgraded during a repair?

Yes. Seals, bearing protection, materials and impeller diameter can all be changed while the pump is apart, usually for far less than a new unit.

Are aftermarket pump parts reliable?

Parts made to the original dimensions and materials by an established manufacturer perform as the originals do. Ask what the part is made of and who made it.
