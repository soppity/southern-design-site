Pumps and control valves for water and wastewater

What the duty demands

Water and wastewater systems move large volumes over long distances, often with pumps starting and stopping many times a day. Suction conditions, surge on long pipelines and solids in the flow are the recurring problems.

What we supply

  • Split case and vertical turbine pumps: American Marsh, and Aurora and Fairbanks in Maryland, DC and Delaware
  • Submersible pumps: Ebara in Arkansas, Myers in Maryland, DC and Delaware
  • Hisco self-priming trash pumps
  • Cla-Val automatic control valves and NIBCO isolation and check valves
  • Blacoh surge suppressors

Problems we are called about

  • Water hammer and pulsation in pump systems: causes and fixes
  • Pump cavitation: signs, causes and how to stop it
  • Should you repair or replace an industrial pump?
