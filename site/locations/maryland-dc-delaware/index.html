Pumps, seals, valves and pump repair in Maryland, DC and Delaware

Southern Design's East Coast office is in Millersville, Maryland, between Baltimore and Annapolis. We supply and support pumps, mechanical seals and valves for plants and utilities across Maryland, Washington DC and Delaware.
