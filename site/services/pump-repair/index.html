Industrial pump repair, with the cause written down

Precision repair for pumps and other rotating equipment, including magnetic drive and mechanically sealed pumps. Every repair comes with the cause of failure, so you know what to change, not just what was replaced.

What is included

  • Repair of a wide variety of pumps and other rotating equipment
  • Magnetic drive and sealed pump repair
  • Mechanical seal repair and exchange programs
  • Pump and mechanical seal root cause failure analysis and reporting
  • On-site performance troubleshooting
  • Lubrication analysis and upgrade recommendations

How a repair runs

  1. Tell us the make and model and what happened.
  2. We inspect the pump and report what failed and why.
  3. You approve the scope, including any upgrades worth making while it is apart.
  4. We repair it.
  5. The repair and its cause go into the pump's history. Contract customers see this in the Reliability Management System.

Signs a pump needs more than a new part

  • The same seal or bearing has been replaced more than once in a year. See why seals keep failing and why bearings fail early.
  • It is noisy or vibrating. See pump cavitation.
  • It no longer makes its flow or pressure. See running off the curve.
  • You are unsure whether it is worth fixing. See repair or replace.

Frequently asked questions

What types of pump do you repair?

A wide variety of pumps and rotating equipment, including magnetic drive and mechanically sealed pumps. Send us the make and model and we will confirm.

Will you tell us why the pump failed?

Yes. Root cause failure analysis and reporting is part of the service for pumps and mechanical seals.

Can you troubleshoot on site before the pump is pulled?

Yes. On-site performance troubleshooting is part of the service, and field service is available by the day or turnkey.
